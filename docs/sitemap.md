# EaleyImages — Site Structure & Navigation Plan

## Primary navigation

```
Home  |  Portfolio  |  Services & Pricing  |  Prints & Albums  |  About  |  Contact
```

Keep the nav to six items max — this is standard for a premium/editorial
photography site and matches the "clean, minimal" brand direction. Client
galleries (CloudSpot) are linked from Contact/footer, not the main nav —
those links are personal to each client, not a public site page.

## Page-by-page structure

### 1. Home
- Hero (full-bleed image, headline, subheadline, two CTAs)
- Brief intro / philosophy (2–3 sentences, links to About)
- Services overview grid (Portraits · Families/Groups · Grad & Prom ·
  Corporate · Events · Mini Sessions) — each tile links to its anchor on
  the Services & Pricing page
- Portfolio teaser (curated grid of 6–9 standout images, mixed categories)
  linking to the full Portfolio page
- Social proof placeholder (testimonial quote — add once collected)
- Closing CTA band ("Book a Session")

### 2. Portfolio
Category-based galleries (see `content/portfolio-structure.md` for the
full breakdown and image-sourcing plan):
- Portraits
- Families & Groups
- Grad & Prom
- Corporate & Branding
- Events (general/lifestyle + sports/cycling)
- Mini Sessions

Squarespace implementation: one Portfolio landing page with a category
filter/grid, OR a Portfolio parent page with child gallery pages per
category (cleaner for SEO — each category gets its own URL/title/meta).
Recommend the child-page approach given the SEO plan.

### 3. Services & Pricing
Single scrollable page with anchored sections per category (Portraits,
Families/Groups, Grad & Prom, Corporate, Events, Mini Sessions, Add-Ons,
Booking & Payment Policy). See `content/pricing.md` for full copy.
Anchor links from the Home services grid jump straight to each section.

### 4. Prints & Albums
Standalone page (or Squarespace Store section) showcasing the print/album
product line as an upsell — separate from session pricing since it's
post-session purchasing, not booking. See `content/prints-albums.md`.

### 5. About
- Photographer bio/philosophy (expanded from brand positioning)
- Process: what a session/event day looks like
- Gear & approach (Fujifilm system, editing style) — brief, credibility-
  building, not overly technical
- Service area statement (Cary-based, RTP + Triangle, travel available)
- CTA to Contact/Book

### 6. Contact
- Short intro + response-time expectation
- Contact form (name, email, session type dropdown, date, message)
- Direct email: lannyealeyphotography@gmail.com
- Link to booking policy (anchor to Services & Pricing)
- Service area note

## Footer (site-wide)
- Logo mark
- Nav links (repeat of primary nav)
- Contact email
- Service area line ("Based in Cary, NC · Serving RTP & the Triangle ·
  Travel available")
- Social links (Instagram, etc. — add when available)
- Client Gallery Login link (CloudSpot)

## Notes on scope
- Weddings is omitted per confirmed pricing sheet (see source doc note) —
  no Weddings nav item or page for now.
- No blog is included in this pass. A blog is a strong future SEO lever
  (recent session recaps, local venue guides) but wasn't part of the
  current content package — flagging as a later addition, not building it
  now.
