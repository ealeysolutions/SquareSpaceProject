## Bio / Philosophy

*Image: a portrait of Lanny with camera, or a behind-the-scenes shot.*

EaleyImages is built around a simple idea: photography should feel
natural, modern, and beautifully intentional. Using a combination of
medium-format and professional Fujifilm cameras, the work emphasizes
clean, editorial images with true-to-life color and thoughtful
composition. Whether it's a portrait session, a family gathering, a grad
milestone, a corporate headshot, or a full day of event coverage, the
goal is the same: make the experience easy, comfortable, and enjoyable —
and deliver images that hold up over time.

## The Experience

Every session starts with a conversation — what the moment means, who's
involved, and what feels true to you. From there, it's about showing up
prepared, reading the light and the room, and staying out of the way
enough for real moments to happen. The result: clean, editorial images
you'll actually want to print, frame, and pass down.

## Gear & Approach

Sessions are shot on a professional Fujifilm system — including the GFX
100S II for premium, medium-format work — paired with natural or
studio-quality lighting depending on the setting. The editing style stays
clean and true-to-life: minimal, natural color, no heavy filters or
over-processing.

## Service Area

Based in Cary, NC, and shooting throughout RTP (Research Triangle Park)
and the greater Triangle area, with travel available for events and
sessions further out.
